Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Who this applies to

Daily Miniature Workflow is a personal, single-user automation. Its author is its only user. It collects no data from anyone else.

What it accesses

With the operator's consent it uses two Google API scopes:

  • drive.file — create and manage only the files this application itself creates in the operator's Google Drive. It cannot read or modify any other file in that Drive.
  • spreadsheets — read and write the tracking spreadsheet this application creates.

What it stores

Generated videos, thumbnails, and text are written to the operator's own Google Drive and Google Sheet. No copy is stored on any server operated by the author. No analytics, cookies, or tracking are used on this site.

Third parties

Text prompts are sent to the Google Gemini API and the OpenAI API to generate content, and are handled under those providers' respective policies. No personal information is included in those requests.

Retention and deletion

All output lives in the operator's Google account and is deleted by deleting it there. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Reachable at the support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
